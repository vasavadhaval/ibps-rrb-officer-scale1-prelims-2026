# IBPS RRB Officer Scale-I Prelims 2026

## Goal

Prepare for the Officer Scale-I Preliminary Examination scheduled for 21-22 November 2026.

## Official exam structure

- Reasoning Ability: 40 questions, 40 marks, 25 minutes
- Quantitative Aptitude: 40 questions, 40 marks, 20 minutes
- Total: 80 questions, 80 marks, 45 minutes
- Penalty: one-fourth of the marks assigned to a question for each wrong answer
- Both sections must be qualified.

## How this repository is organised

1. `01_Exam_Pattern_and_Strategy.md` - official structure and attempt strategy.
2. `02_Reasoning_Topic_Map.md` - complete Reasoning inventory and learning order.
3. `03_Quant_Topic_Map.md` - complete Quant inventory and prerequisites.
4. `04_Previous_Year_Question_Trends.md` - memory-based 2024-2025 trends, not official weightage.
5. `05_59_Day_Daily_Plan.md` - assignment for every day up to the exam.
6. Date folders - the actual lesson, questions and answer review for that day.

## Daily workflow

1. Read `00_Aaj_Ka_Plan.md` in the current date folder.
2. Study the Reasoning lesson and solve its practice set.
3. Study the Quant lesson and solve its practice set.
4. Record incorrect questions in the mistake log.
5. Redo mistakes the following day without looking at the solution.

## Main rule

Do not measure progress only by completed chapters. Measure it using timed accuracy:

- Foundation target: at least 85% accuracy without rushing.
- Development target: at least 90% accuracy under sectional timing.
- Final target: stable mock performance with careful question selection.

# Aaj Ka Plan - 23 September 2026

## Total study time: approximately 3 hours 20 minutes

### 1. Calculation warm-up - 15 minutes

- Revise tables 1-20.
- Revise squares 1-25.
- Write common fractions from 1/2 to 1/10 as percentages.

### 2. Reasoning - Inequality - 50 minutes

- Understand symbols and comparison chains.
- Learn definite, indefinite and either-or conclusions.
- Solve 10 untimed examples.
- Solve 20 questions with a 12-minute timer.
- Record every incorrect conclusion in the mistake log.

### 3. Quant - Simplification - 50 minutes

- Revise BODMAS.
- Revise fraction, decimal and percentage conversion.
- Solve 10 untimed examples.
- Solve 25 questions with a 15-minute timer.
- Mark every slow calculation for revision.

### 4. Timed mini test - 30 minutes

- Inequality: 15 questions in 10 minutes.
- Simplification: 20 questions in 15 minutes.
- Use the final 5 minutes to check unattempted questions.

### 5. Analysis - 30 minutes

For every error, write:

- Question number
- Your answer
- Correct answer
- Error type
- Correct method

### Completion standard

- Minimum 85% accuracy in both topics.
- If accuracy is below 85%, repeat 10 similar questions before moving to Day 2.

# Quant - Simplification: Study Scope

## Concepts to learn

- BODMAS order
- Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division of fractions
- Decimal operations
- Percentage conversion
- Squares and square roots used in common banking questions
- Basic approximation only after exact simplification is understood

## Required calculation memory

- Tables 1-20 today; extend to 30 during the week
- Squares 1-25 today; extend to 40
- Common fractions: 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5, 1/6, 1/8, 1/10

## Practice target

- 10 basic questions without a timer
- 25 standard questions in 15 minutes
- 20-question mini test in 15 minutes
- Minimum accuracy: 85% today, progressing to 90%+

## Common errors to watch

- Ignoring brackets
- Multiplying before resolving division from left to right
- Decimal-place mistakes
- Cancelling terms across addition or subtraction
- Rushing before identifying an easier factorisation

# Reasoning - Inequality: Study Scope

## Concepts to learn

- Meaning of `>`, `<`, `>=`, `<=` and `=`
- Building a comparison chain
- Definite true and definite false conclusions
- Conclusions that cannot be determined
- Either-or conclusions
- Coded inequalities

## Required skill

Convert every statement into one continuous chain whenever possible. Never reverse a comparison sign accidentally.

## Practice target

- 10 basic questions without a timer
- 20 standard questions in 12 minutes
- 15-question mini test in 10 minutes
- Minimum accuracy: 85% today, progressing to 90%+

## Common errors to watch

- Treating `>=` as strictly greater than
- Assuming a relationship that is not connected through the chain
- Reversing the direction when reading from right to left
- Marking a possible conclusion as definitely true

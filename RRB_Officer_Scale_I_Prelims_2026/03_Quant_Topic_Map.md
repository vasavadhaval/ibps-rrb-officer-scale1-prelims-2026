# Quantitative Aptitude - Complete Topic Map

## Foundation: calculation speed

- Tables from 1 to 30
- Squares from 1 to 40
- Cubes from 1 to 20
- Fraction-to-percentage conversions
- Percentage-to-fraction conversions
- Multiplication and division shortcuts
- BODMAS

Calculation practice must continue daily even after the foundation week.

## Priority A: speed topics

- Simplification
- Approximation
- Missing number series
- Wrong number series
- Quadratic equations

## Priority B: arithmetic foundation

- Percentage
- Ratio and proportion
- Average
- Profit, loss and discount
- Simple interest
- Compound interest
- Partnership
- Ages

## Priority C: applied arithmetic

- Time and work
- Pipes and cisterns
- Time, speed and distance
- Trains
- Boats and streams
- Mixture and allegation
- Mensuration
- Number system, HCF and LCM

## Priority D: data interpretation

- Table DI
- Bar graph DI
- Line graph DI
- Pie chart DI
- Caselet DI
- Missing-table DI
- Mixed DI

DI depends on percentages, ratios, averages and fast calculations. Learn those prerequisites before attempting difficult sets.

## Lower-frequency or variable topics

- Data sufficiency
- Quantity comparison
- Probability
- Permutation and combination

Do not spend early preparation days on low-frequency advanced topics while core arithmetic, series, quadratic equations or DI remain weak.

## Learning order

1. Calculation basics and BODMAS
2. Simplification and approximation
3. Number series
4. Quadratic equations
5. Percentage
6. Ratio and proportion
7. Average
8. Profit-loss and interest
9. Partnership and ages
10. Time-work and pipes
11. Speed-distance, trains and boats
12. Mixture and mensuration
13. Data interpretation
14. Mixed timed practice

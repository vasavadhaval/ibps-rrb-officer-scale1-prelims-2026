# Previous-Year Question Trends

## Important limitation

IBPS does not publish an official chapter-wise syllabus or official question paper after every shift. The distributions below come from candidate-reported, memory-based exam analyses. Treat them as preparation guidance, not guaranteed 2026 weightage.

## 2025 Reasoning trend

Reported shifts were heavily puzzle-oriented. Common distributions included:

- Approximately 18-25 questions from seating arrangements and puzzles.
- Approximately 3-5 questions each from several direct topics such as syllogism, blood relation, direction, ranking or coding-decoding.
- Puzzle formats included linear, circular, floor/flat, age, day/month, designation and stack arrangements.

Preparation implication: direct topics provide faster marks, but avoiding puzzles entirely would leave too much of the paper untouched.

## 2025 Quant trend

Across reported shifts, the recurring structure was:

- Data interpretation: commonly 10-16 questions.
- Arithmetic/application problems: commonly 12-14 questions.
- Number series: commonly 5-6 questions.
- Quadratic equations: commonly 5-6 questions.
- Approximation appeared in several shifts, commonly around 5-6 questions, but was not reported identically in every shift.

Preparation implication: arithmetic and DI together can form more than half of Quant. Series, quadratic equations and approximation are important speed-scoring areas when present.

## 2024 comparison

Memory-based analyses also reported roughly 20 or more puzzle/seating questions in Reasoning and around 15-16 DI questions in some Quant shifts. This supports the same core preparation priorities:

1. Fast direct Reasoning topics
2. Core puzzle types
3. Calculation speed
4. Arithmetic fundamentals
5. Data interpretation
